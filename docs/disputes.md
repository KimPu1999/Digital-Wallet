# Disputes (Phase 11)

Disputes are complaint/investigation records separate from provider chargeback state and support tickets. They have their own reference, events, assignments, statuses, and resolution notes. Creating or resolving a dispute never posts a reversal, edits a ledger entry, or changes a payment intent.

Customer dispute access is owner-scoped. Platform staff access requires support permissions and MFA. Jurisdiction-specific complaint and consumer-protection procedures remain production prerequisites.
