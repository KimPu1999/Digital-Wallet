# Pilot Eligibility and Limits

Eligibility is an explicit deny-by-default boundary for required checks. Country capabilities determine whether KYC is required; an approved KYC case is required when configured. Merchants require an active/approved account and approved KYB verification.

Pilot limits use integer minor units and are currency-specific. Supported controls include per-transaction amount, daily volume, daily activity, transfer frequency, merchant payment frequency and FX conversion frequency. The pilot limit is not a regulatory threshold; the stricter applicable risk or compliance control must still apply.
