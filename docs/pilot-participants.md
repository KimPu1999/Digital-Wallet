# Pilot Participants and Cohorts

Cohorts are scoped to a pilot and may contain customers, merchants or explicitly identified internal testers. Enrollment references existing `users` or `merchants`; it does not create a second identity system.

Enrollment is unique per pilot and subject. Eligibility checks cover sandbox environment, pilot schedule and status, account authorization, email verification, KYC/KYB requirements and open high-risk restrictions. Failed checks leave the participant invited and ineligible.

Participant access is always evaluated server-side. Frontend cohort membership or hidden controls are not authorization.
