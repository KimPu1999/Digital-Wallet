# Regulatory readiness

Phase 18 adds evidence-backed regulatory readiness records. The platform does not determine licensing requirements, provide legal advice, or claim authorization. Jurisdiction and activity assessments require qualified legal review and source references.

The readiness domain covers jurisdictions, regulated activities, licensing records, obligations, compliance assessments, assurance findings, private evidence references, and controlled pilot planning. Missing evidence remains `not_started`, `evidence_pending`, or `blocked`; it is never treated as approval.

All responses are sandbox-labelled and production financial execution is disabled.
